# Copyright and DMCA policy

We respect the rights of people who create things, and we expect members of {{app_name}} to do the same. Only post photos, videos, music, writing and other work that you made yourself or have permission to use. This policy explains how to tell us about content that infringes your copyright or trademark, and what happens next.

Last updated: {{effective_date}}

## Quick report in the app

If you see something on {{app_name}} that uses your work without permission, open the menu on the post, Short, story, Memory, comment or profile, choose **Report**, then **Copyright or trademark**. This tells our team to look at it.

A quick report doesn't contain everything the law asks for, so if you own the rights (or act for the owner) and want the content taken down, please also send a formal notice as described below.

## Sending a copyright notice (DMCA)

Under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512), your notice must include:

- your physical or electronic signature, as the copyright owner or someone authorised to act for them;
- a description of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed (or, if several works are covered by one notice, a representative list);
- a description of the material you say is infringing and where it is on {{app_name}}, with enough detail for us to find it, such as the member's username, the date it was posted and a description or link;
- your name, postal address, telephone number and email address;
- a statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
- a statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act on their behalf.

Send your notice to our designated agent:

{{dmca_agent}}

Email: [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}), with "Copyright notice" in the subject line.

Please be accurate. Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly makes a false claim that material is infringing can be liable for damages, including costs and legal fees. Before sending a notice, consider whether the use could be allowed by law, for example as fair use or fair dealing.

## What happens next

When we receive a complete notice, we'll act promptly to remove the content or stop it being seen. We'll tell the member who posted it, and we may give them a copy of the notice, including your name and contact details, so they can respond. We may not act on notices that are incomplete or that appear to be abusive.

## Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe this was a mistake, or that you have the right to use it, you can send us a counter-notice. It must include:

- your physical or electronic signature;
- a description of the content that was removed and where it appeared before it was removed;
- a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good faith belief that the content was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification;
- your name, postal address and telephone number;
- a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the US Federal District Court for the judicial district where your address is (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of legal papers from the person who sent the original notice, or their agent.

Send it to our designated agent at the address above. We'll pass it to the person who sent the original notice. If they don't tell us within 10 business days that they have started court proceedings to stop the infringement, we'll restore the content between 10 and 14 business days after we received your counter-notice.

## Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of members who repeatedly infringe others' rights. As a rule, an account that receives three valid copyright notices within 12 months, which are not successfully challenged by counter-notice, will be closed. We may act sooner where an infringement is serious or deliberate.

## UK and EU notices

If you're in the UK or the EU, you can also tell us about content you believe is illegal, including copyright and trademark infringement, under the notice-and-takedown rules that apply there. Send us:

- an explanation of why you believe the content is illegal;
- exactly where it is on {{app_name}} (the member's username and a description or link);
- your name and email address;
- a statement that you believe in good faith that the information in your notice is accurate and complete.

We'll look at your notice promptly and carefully, tell you what we decided, and tell the member whose content is affected, with our reasons. Either of you can ask us to look at the decision again by replying to our message.

## Trademarks

If an account or content uses your trademark in a way that is likely to confuse people about who is behind it, tell us at {{contact_email}}. Include your name and contact details, the trademark and where it is registered (with the registration number), the account or content concerned, and how it is likely to cause confusion. We may remove the content, or change or reclaim a username, where it infringes a trademark.

## Other concerns

For other problems with content, such as privacy, harassment or impersonation, use **Report** in the app or contact us at {{contact_email}}. See our [Terms of use](/terms) and [Community guidelines](/community-guidelines).
