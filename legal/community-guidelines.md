# Community guidelines

{{app_name}} is a place for the Seventh-day Adventist church family to share faith, encourage one another and stay connected. "Let your conversation be always full of grace" (Colossians 4:6). These guidelines help keep it that way.

Last updated: {{effective_date}}

## Be kind

- Treat others the way you would like to be treated.
- Disagreement is welcome, about ideas and even beliefs, when it is respectful. Don't attack, mock or belittle people.
- No bullying, harassment, threats or hate, including against people of other faiths, denominations or none.

## Keep it fit for church

- No nudity or sexual content, and no graphic violence.
- Don't promote alcohol, tobacco, vaping, drugs or gambling.
- Offensive words are hidden automatically in comments and captions. Please don't try to get around that.

## Protect children and anyone vulnerable

- Never share anything that sexualises or endangers a child. We report it to the authorities.
- Ask a parent's permission before posting photos or videos of other people's children.
- Read our [Child safety standards](/child-safety) to see how we protect younger members.

## Be honest

- Be yourself. Don't pretend to be someone else, or to speak for a church or organisation you don't represent.
- No scams, fake fundraisers or false claims that could hurt people, for example about health or money.
- If you promote a post or run a fundraiser, describe it truthfully.

## Respect privacy

- Don't share other people's personal details, private messages or photos without their permission.
- Prayer requests about others are best shared with their agreement.

## Share what's yours

Only post photos, videos, music and writing you made or have permission to use. See our [Copyright and DMCA policy](/copyright).

## No spam

Don't post the same thing again and again, send unwanted messages, or use automated tools. Keep selling and advertising to the Promote feature.

## If something isn't right

Use **Report** on any post, Short, story, Memory, comment or account, and **Block** anyone you don't want to hear from. Our team reviews every report. Depending on what happened, we may remove content, limit features, or suspend or close an account. Serious harm and anything involving children is reported to the authorities.
