# Child safety standards

{{app_name}} is a place for church families, and keeping children and young people safe matters more to us than anything else in the app. These standards explain who can join, how we protect younger members, and how to report a concern.

Last updated: {{effective_date}}

## Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

We do not allow any content or behaviour that sexualises, abuses or exploits children. This includes:

- child sexual abuse material of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated images, and "jokes";
- grooming: building a relationship with a child in order to abuse or exploit them;
- asking a child for sexual images or conversations, or threatening to share images (sextortion);
- sexualised comments about children, or content that encourages others to harm them;
- trafficking or arranging to meet a child for sexual purposes.

When we find this, we remove it, close the accounts involved, keep the evidence the law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the authorities, including the National Crime Agency in the UK, as the law requires.

## Who can join

- You must be at least 13 to use {{app_name}}.
- We ask for a date of birth first: on the sign-up page before an email address, password or Apple or Google sign-in, and for anyone who signs in with Apple or Google, before a profile is made. The date isn't filled in for you, and nothing is saved until the age check is passed.
- If the date given is under 13, no account is created. The phone remembers this for 24 hours, so entering a different date straight away doesn't work.
- If an existing account gives a date of birth under 13, it is closed straight away, can't be restored, and is deleted with everything in it within two days.
- If we learn in any other way that a member is under 13, we close the account.

## Protections for members under 14

- Their account is always private: only people they approve see their posts and stories.
- Posts marked as sensitive are hidden from them.
- They can only message and call people they follow, and only people they follow can message or call them.
- They can't broadcast live, share their location, run fundraisers or offer subscriptions.
- Their date of birth, like everyone's, is seen only by them and our admins.

## Tools for everyone

- **Report** any post, Short, story, Memory, comment or account from its menu.
- **Block** an account: neither of you will see the other's content, and they can't message you.
- Make your account **private**, and share stories with **close friends** only.
- Live location is **off** unless you switch it on, and you choose who sees it.

## How to report a concern

- **In the app**: use Report on the content or the account. We treat reports about children as the most urgent.
- **By email**: write to [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}) with "Child safety" in the subject line. Tell us the username and what you saw, but please don't send us any images of abuse.
- **If a child is in immediate danger**, call the emergency services straight away: 999 in the UK, 911 in the US.

You can also report directly:

- **UK**: images of child sexual abuse to the Internet Watch Foundation at [report.iwf.org.uk](https://report.iwf.org.uk), and grooming or online sexual abuse to CEOP at [ceop.police.uk](https://www.ceop.police.uk/Safety-Centre/). Children can talk to Childline on 0800 1111, and adults with a concern about a child can call the NSPCC on 0808 800 5000.
- **US**: the NCMEC CyberTipline at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) or 1-800-843-5678.

## Working with the authorities

We cooperate with law enforcement and child protection organisations. We respond to valid legal requests, and we share information without waiting for a request when we believe a child is at risk of serious harm.

## For parents and guardians

If you believe your child under 13 has an account, or you have any concern about your child on {{app_name}}, email [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}). We'll close the account of a child under 13 and delete their information. Your phone's own controls, such as Screen Time on iPhone, can also limit which apps your child can use.
