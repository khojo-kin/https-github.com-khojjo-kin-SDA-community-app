## What we collect

### Information you give us

- **Your account.** Your email address and password, or the name and email address Apple or Google share when you sign in with them. A phone number if you add one for sign-in codes. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk: we never see them.
- **Your date of birth.** We ask everyone for it before an account is made. It is never shown on your profile. Only you and our admins can see it.
- **Your profile.** Your name, username, photo, bio, website, pronouns and gender if you add them, your church if you choose one, and a contact email or phone number if you choose to show one. Your profile also shows the country where your account is based, which we take from where you first signed in.
- **What you share.** Posts, Shorts, stories, Memories, captions, comments, likes, saves, reposts, notes, live broadcasts and the people you mention or tag.
- **Your messages.** Text, photos, videos, voice messages, view-once photos, shared posts and call records in your chats.
- **Live location.** Only if you switch it on, and only while it is on (see below).
- **Things you send us.** Support chats, feedback and reviews, ideas, problem reports, and the reports you make about content or accounts.
- **Identity checks.** If you apply for the blue tick, a photo of an identity document and a selfie. Our admins compare them by eye; we don't use automated face recognition.
- **Payments.** If you buy Community Plus, promote a post, give to a fundraiser or subscribe to a creator, Stripe collects your card details. Card details never reach our servers. We receive the result of the payment and, for saved cards, the card brand, the last four digits and the expiry date so you can recognise them.
- **Payouts.** If you run a fundraiser or offer subscriptions, you set up a payout account with Stripe, which collects the identity and bank details it needs directly from you.

### Information created as you use the app

- **Activity status.** When you were last in the app, shown as "Active now" only if you allow it in your settings.
- **Views.** Who has viewed your stories, how many times a post or Short has been viewed, and the latest time each member viewed a profile. Community Plus members can see who viewed their profile in the last 7 days.
- **Sign-in activity.** The devices you're signed in on and their approximate location (city and country, worked out from the internet address), which you can see in Settings. Clerk keeps this for us.
- **Safety records.** Counts of how often an account does certain things (such as posting or messaging), used to stop spam and abuse. These are cleared after two days.
- **Technical logs.** Our hosting providers keep short-lived logs of requests, such as internet addresses and times, to keep the service running and secure.

### Information from others

- Apple or Google, when you sign in with them.
- Stripe, about whether a payment, subscription or payout succeeded.
- Other members, for example when they mention you, add you to their close friends, message you or report you.

### What stays on your phone

Your settings (such as theme, language and camera choices), copies of recent screens so the app opens quickly, your sign-in token, and a record of the age check described in our [Child safety standards](/child-safety). The app only sees the photos and videos you choose, and only uses your microphone and camera when you record, call or go live.

## How we use it, and our lawful bases

UK data protection law requires us to have a lawful basis for each use of your information. These are ours.

- **To provide the app you asked for**: your account, profile, posts, Shorts, stories, messages, calls, live broadcasts, notifications in the app, Erha, captions and support. Basis: performing our contract with you.
- **To keep members safe and the service secure**: checking ages, applying the protections for members under 14, reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our rules, limiting spam, showing your sign-in activity, and preventing fraud. Basis: our legitimate interests in running a safe service, and our legal obligations.
- **To take payments and keep financial records.** Basis: our contract with you, and our legal obligations under tax and accounting law.
- **To verify identities for the blue tick.** Basis: your consent, which you give by applying. You can withdraw it before a decision by deleting your request.
- **To share your live location.** Basis: your consent. You can switch it off at any time.
- **To answer you and improve the app** from your support chats, feedback and reviews. Basis: our legitimate interests.
- **To comply with the law**, respond to valid legal requests, and establish or defend legal claims. Basis: legal obligation, or our legitimate interests.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights. You can object (see "Your rights" below).

**Religious beliefs.** {{app_name}} is an app for the Seventh-day Adventist church family, so using it, choosing a church, or what you post may reveal your religious beliefs, which the law treats as special category information. We only use this to provide the app. We rely on your explicit consent for the church you choose to add (you can remove it at any time in Edit profile), and, for what you publish on your profile, on the fact that you have chosen to make it public.

**Automated decisions.** If the date of birth given for an account is under 13, the account is closed automatically, because we can't provide the service to children under 13. If you think this happened by mistake, contact us and a person will look at it. We don't make any other decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. Our systems do automatically hide offensive words and limit how often an account can do some things.

We don't send marketing emails. The emails you get from us are about your account, such as sign-in codes.

## Who can see what

- Everything on {{app_name}} needs you to be signed in. Nothing is visible to people who aren't members.
- Your profile, posts and Shorts are visible to all members unless your account is private, in which case only the followers you approve see your posts and stories. Accounts of members under 14 are always private.
- Stories are visible for 24 hours, to everyone who can see your posts or to your close friends only, as you choose.
- Messages can only be opened in the app by the people in the chat. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on our servers so they reach the other person. We don't read your messages, and access by the people who run the service is limited to what is needed to keep it working, to investigate a serious safety report, or to comply with the law.
- Your live location is visible only to the audience you choose (people you follow who follow you back, or your close friends), only while it's on, and never to members you've blocked or who have blocked you.
- Photos, videos and voice messages are stored at long, random web addresses that can't be guessed. Anyone who is given the exact address of a file can open that file, so please don't share those addresses. View-once photos are handed only to the person they were sent to, at the moment they open them, and the file is then deleted within about ten minutes.
- Our admins can see reports, identity check photos, support chats, dates of birth and payment records, so they can keep members safe and help you. They can't edit your profile.

## AI features

Some features send information to AI services that work for us.

- **Erha**, the church assistant, sends what you write to it, with your recent messages to Erha for context, to Anthropic's Claude to write a reply. Your Erha conversation is kept so you can come back to it, until you clear it or delete your account.
- **Support chats** are answered by an assistant built on Anthropic's Claude until someone from our team joins. Our team can read support chats.
- **Captions** for Shorts are made by sending the video's sound to OpenAI's speech-to-text service (Whisper). When someone reads captions in another language, the caption text is translated by Claude.

Under their business terms, neither Anthropic nor OpenAI uses what we send them to train their models by default. Please don't share more personal information with Erha or the support assistant than you need to.

### Others

- **Call and live video connections.** Calls and live broadcasts go directly between phones. To help them connect, phones contact Google's STUN servers and, when a network blocks a direct connection, the Open Relay service run by Metered, which passes the encrypted audio and video through without storing it. Both see the internet addresses involved. Some older call links in chats open Jitsi Meet, which handles that call under its own privacy policy.
- **Bible, Sabbath School and hymn content.** The app fetches Bible chapters from bible-api.com, Sabbath School lessons from Adventech, and some hymn words from hymnary.org. These services see your phone's internet address and what was requested, but nothing about your account.

- **Authorities**, when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm. We report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the relevant authorities, as described in our [Child safety standards](/child-safety).
- **Professional advisers** such as lawyers and accountants, under a duty of confidentiality.
- **A new owner**, if {{app_name}} is ever transferred. We would tell you first, and this policy would continue to protect your information.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and content**: while your account is open. You can delete posts, stories, Memories, comments and messages at any time; deleting a message removes it for you.
- **Deleted accounts**: when you delete your account it is hidden at once, and 30 days later your profile, content, messages and files are permanently deleted. You can restore it by signing in before then.
- **Accounts closed because the owner is under 13**: deleted with everything in them within two days.
- **Stories**: shown for 24 hours, then kept in your private archive until you delete them or your account. Notes last 24 hours.
- **View-once photos**: deleted within about ten minutes of being opened.
- **Live location**: deleted as soon as you switch it off. Others stop seeing it 24 hours after it last updated.
- **Identity check photos**: deleted as soon as an admin has made a decision.
- **Erha conversations**: until you clear them or delete your account.
- **Reports**: kept while they are needed to keep members safe and to deal with repeated problems. A report is deleted if the account that made it is deleted.
- **Payment records**: our record of each payment (amount, date and type) is kept for six years for tax and accounting law, with the link to your account removed when your account is deleted. Stripe keeps its own records under its policy.
- **Safety counts**: two days. **Technical logs**: a short period set by our providers.

Deleted information can remain in our providers' backups for a short time before it is overwritten. We may keep something longer if the law requires it, or if it is needed for a legal claim or an investigation into serious harm.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **access** your information and get a copy of it;
- **correct** information that is wrong or incomplete;
- **delete** your information;
- **restrict** how we use it, or **object** to uses based on our legitimate interests;
- **take your information with you** in a commonly used electronic format;
- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it, at any time (this doesn't affect what we did before);
- ask for a person to review a decision made **automatically**.

**How to use them.** Much of this you can do in the app: edit your profile, delete what you've shared, switch off features in Settings, and delete your account in Settings › Account. For anything else, including a copy of your information, email {{contact_email}} from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it's you. We don't charge, and we reply within one month (we can extend this by two months for complex requests, and will tell you if we do).

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint), phone 0303 123 1113. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

## Children and teenagers

You must be at least 13 to use {{app_name}}. We ask for a date of birth before an account is made, and we don't create accounts for anyone under 13. Members under 14 get extra protections: their account is always private, sensitive posts are hidden from them, they can only message and call people they follow, and they can't go live, share their location, run fundraisers or offer subscriptions. We don't profile anyone for advertising. Our [Child safety standards](/child-safety) explain more.

## How we keep it safe

- Information travels to and from the app encrypted (HTTPS), and our providers encrypt it where it is stored.
- Our database has rules that decide, for every request, what each member is allowed to see and change.
- Identity check photos are kept in private storage that only you and our admins can open.
- Card details are handled by Stripe and never touch our servers. Passwords are handled by Clerk and we never see them.
- New devices can be asked to confirm a sign-in with a code, and you can sign out other devices from Settings.

No service is perfectly secure. If you think your account or information is at risk, tell us at {{contact_email}}. If a breach puts your rights at high risk, we will tell you, and we will tell the ICO when the law requires.

## Cookies and similar technologies

**The app** doesn't use cookies, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or session recording. It stores on your phone only what it needs to work (your sign-in, your settings, recent screens and the age check record), which doesn't need your consent. Some pages that open inside the app are run by others, such as Stripe's payout set-up and the Apple and Google sign-in pages, and may use their own cookies under their own policies.

**Our website** doesn't use cookies. If we switch on visitor counting, the site will ask you first, and only with your permission will it count visits using Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites. Your choice is remembered on your device, and you can change it at any time from the link at the bottom of each page.
