Chat with us in the app

The fastest way to get help is inside Community. Go to Settings, then Help, and tap Chat with support. Our assistant answers straight away, at any time of day. If it can’t help, someone from our team joins the same chat, so you never have to explain twice.

From the same screen you can also report a problem, suggest an idea, or see earlier messages with the team.

Email us

If you can’t open the app or sign in, email {{contact_email}}. Tell us the email address or username on your account and what happened, and we’ll reply by email.

Report a person or a post

Tap the three dots on a post, comment or profile, choose Report and say what is wrong. An admin looks at every report. You can also block an account from its profile: they won’t see you, and you won’t see them.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. For concerns about a child, read our child safety standards. To report a copyright problem, see report copyright infringement.

Common questions

How do I get Community?

{{availability_long}}

Who answers when I chat with support?

Our assistant answers straight away, at any time of day. If it can’t help, someone from our team joins the same chat, and you’ll see their name on their replies.

How do I set up my account, or add another one?

Sign in with your email, your phone number, or Apple or Google. Your username and photo are under Settings, then Account. You can add a second account from the same place and switch between them without signing out.

What does a private account do?

Only the people who follow you see your posts and stories. Anyone else who taps Follow sends you a request, which you accept or decline from Notifications. Turn it on or off under Settings, then Account.

Where are the Bible, Hymnal and Pod?

Tap the small floating church button on your screen. It opens the Bible, Hymnal, Pod, Sabbath School, Churches, Communities, Erha and the map from anywhere. Drag it to either side if it’s in the way.

What is Community Plus?

An optional membership: £2.99 a month or £28.49 a year, and your first week is free. It gives you Erha without limits, app fonts, chat wallpapers, your own ring colour and profile theme, custom Memories covers, profile viewers, and sharing your own songs and podcasts on Pod. Subscribe or cancel under Settings, then Community Plus.

How do I get the blue tick?

The blue tick shows an account is who it says it is. It’s free and can’t be bought. Go to Settings, then Request verification, and send a photo of an ID document and a selfie. An admin checks them, and the photos are deleted once your request is decided.

What is Erha?

The church assistant built into the app. Ask it about a Bible passage, a hymn, this week’s lesson or anything about church life. Everyone gets 15 messages a day; Plus members have no limit.

Who can use Community?

Anyone aged 13 or over. Members who are 13 have a private account and extra protections until they turn 14. Read more in our child safety standards.

How do I delete my account?

Under Settings, then Account, choose Delete account. Everything is hidden straight away and deleted for good after 30 days. Read the full details.
