Your church family, all week

Church doesn’t stop when the service ends. Community keeps everyone close: the photos from the potluck, the prayer request from Tuesday, the choir’s new recording.

Posts, Shorts and stories

Share photos and posts, watch and make Shorts, the app’s short videos, and post stories that last a day. Keep the ones that matter in Memories on your profile.

Messages, calls and live video

Chat one to one or in groups, send voice notes and view-once photos, and call friends over video. Go live to share a service or a study with your followers.

Prayer and church life

Post a prayer request and pray for others. Find an Adventist church near you, join your church’s community, and give to fundraisers for causes you care about.

Erha, the church assistant

Ask Erha about a Bible passage, a hymn or this week’s lesson, or anything about church life. Everyone gets 15 messages a day.

Safe for young members

Community is for ages 13 and up. Members who are 13 have extra protections until they turn 14:

  • Their account is private, and their birth date is never shown.
  • Only people they follow can message or call them.
  • They don’t see posts marked as sensitive, and they can’t go live.
  • They aren’t suggested to adults as people to follow.

For everyone, an admin reads every report, offensive words are hidden in comments and captions, and anyone can be blocked. Blue ticks are free and given only after an ID check; they can’t be bought.
