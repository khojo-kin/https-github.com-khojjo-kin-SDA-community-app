Send a copyright notice

If you own the copyright in a work, or are authorised to act for the owner, and you believe it has been copied on Community without permission, send a written notice to our designated copyright agent:

Copyright agent: {{dmca_agent}}

Email: {{contact_email}}, with the subject “Copyright notice”

Post: {{operator_name}}, {{postal_address_line}}

What to include

So that we can act on it, your notice needs:

  • your name, postal address, phone number and email address
  • a description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed
  • where the material is on Community: the username of the account, and enough detail about the post, Short, story, song or podcast for us to find it, such as its caption and the date it was posted
  • a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law
  • a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner of the copyright or authorised to act for the owner
  • your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough)

What happens next

We review every notice. If the material infringes, we remove it or block access to it and tell the person who posted it. We may give them a copy of your notice, including your name and contact details, so they can respond; they may send a counter-notice if they believe it was removed by mistake. Accounts that repeatedly infringe are closed. Our copyright and DMCA policy explains counter-notices in full.

Please make sure the material really infringes before you send a notice: knowingly misrepresenting that something infringes can make you liable for damages.

Using the app?

You can also tap the three dots on a post, comment or profile, choose Report and say it uses your work. An admin looks at every report. For a formal notice, use the details above.
