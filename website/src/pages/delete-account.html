Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Community and sign in to the account you want to delete. If you have more than one account, switch to that one first.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Under Delete account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is hidden straight away and you are signed out.

If you have Community Plus, cancel it first under Settings, then Community Plus, so you aren’t charged again.

What is deleted, and when

  1. Straight away

    Your profile, posts, Shorts, stories, Memories, comments and messages are hidden from everyone.

  2. For the next 30 days

    You can change your mind. Sign back in and tap Restore, and everything comes back exactly as it was.

  3. After 30 days

    Your account is deleted for good, with everything you shared: your profile, posts, Shorts, stories, Memories, comments, messages and followers, and the photos, videos and voice notes you uploaded. This can’t be undone.

Some records may be kept for longer where the law requires it, such as records of payments. Our privacy policy explains what is kept and for how long.

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete some of it, without closing your account. Email {{contact_email}} with the subject “Data request” from the email address on your account. We reply within one month.
